🚀 Getting Started

  • Visit the Download page and choose your platform (Android APK or Windows Setup). On Android, enable "Install from unknown sources" in Settings. On Windows, run the Setup wizard.
  • Open LindAV after installation and tap "Sign Up." Enter your email and create a password. You'll receive a confirmation email to activate your account.
  • After signing in, tap the large "Scan" button on the dashboard. LindAV will check all installed apps, files, and system settings. Your first scan may take a few minutes.
  • The dashboard shows your protection status, recent scan results, and threat alerts. Green means all clear, yellow means minor issues found, and red means action is needed.

🛡️ Security Features

  • Go to Settings → Real-time Protection to enable or configure scanning. When enabled, LindAV monitors app installs, downloads, and file changes in the background.
  • The App Scanner checks every installed app for known malware signatures, suspicious behavior, and dangerous permissions. It runs automatically after each app is installed or updated.
  • Enable Safe Browsing under Settings → Web Protection. LindAV will block known phishing sites, malicious URLs, and unsafe downloads across all supported browsers.
  • Privacy Guard monitors which apps access your camera, microphone, location, and contacts. Go to Settings → Privacy Guard to review and control app permissions.

💳 Billing & Subscriptions

  • Open LindAV → Settings → Subscription → "Upgrade to Premium." Choose monthly ($4.99/mo) or annual billing. Premium unlocks Privacy Guard, Anti-Theft, Battery Optimizer, and priority support.
  • Manage your subscription in LindAV → Settings → Subscription, or through Google Play Store subscriptions. You can change plans or cancel at any time.
  • We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all Premium subscriptions. Contact support@jerman.com with your order details to request a refund.
  • We accept credit/debit cards, Google Pay, and PayPal through the Google Play Store. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice — contact our sales team.

🔧 Troubleshooting

  • Try clearing the app cache (Settings → Apps → LindAV → Clear Cache), then restart your device. If the issue persists, reinstall LindAV from the Download page.
  • Close background apps to free up memory, ensure you have at least 500 MB of free storage, and make sure you're connected to the internet for the latest threat database updates.
  • Reduce scan frequency in Settings → Scan Schedule. LindAV is optimized for low battery usage, but frequent full scans on older devices may increase consumption. Try "Smart Scan" mode.
  • Ensure notifications are enabled for LindAV in your device's Settings → Apps → LindAV → Notifications. Also check that LindAV isn't restricted by battery optimization settings.
